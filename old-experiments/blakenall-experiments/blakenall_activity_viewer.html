Reading notes. A “post” here is a distinct Facebook post_id found in the scraped comment data — every post in this export belongs to exactly one group and one category, so the per-group and per-category totals are true partitions of the – posts. Comment/reaction counts are summed from the comments actually scraped for that post, not the post’s full public totals.

To label groups by type: edit blakenall_opinion_data/group_types.csv (columns: group,type, free text) and re-run python3 build_blakenall_activity_viewer.py.
